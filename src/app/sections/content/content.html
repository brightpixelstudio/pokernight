Poker Night Sign-up Form

I am excited about hosting a semi-weekly poker night! It's been a while since I have played Texas Hold'em and I am looking forward to getting back into it. I use to play quite a bit and appreciate your interest!

I have a dining table cover that I bought for Texas Hold'em. I have a mechanical card shuffler and professional poker chips. I have a large TV and can provide music of everyones choice - just connect your phone.

I will provide snacks such as chips, pretzels, and peanuts. Please bring your own drinks (alcoholic or non-alcoholic) and of course, please drink responsibly.

Here are some of the rules. Suggestions are welcome in the comments section below.

  • In the beginning, I will be the dealer. As I get more comfortable, I will rotate the dealer position.

  • There will be No Rake.

  • Buy-In will be $20. As we all get more confortable, this may increase if everyone agrees. Re-buy in's will Not be permitted. If you lose all your chips, you are out of the game.

  • Games will be No-Limit Texas Hold'em.

I am collecting the information below to understand who is interested and what date, day and time works the best. Majority rules. I will email the results to each person interested.

After submitting the form it will open your email with the results that you entered. Please submit that email and I will receive it.
